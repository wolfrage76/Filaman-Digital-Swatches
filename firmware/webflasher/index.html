CrowPanel H2C — USB Flasher

Flash the display firmware over the USB cable, straight from your browser.

  1. Connect the CrowPanel to this computer with a USB cable.
  2. Click Install below and pick the CH340 serial port.

Requires Chrome or Edge (Web Serial), the CH340 USB-serial driver, and this page served over HTTPS or http://localhost. This flashes over USB; for updates once the panel is on WiFi, use the on-device page at http://crowpanel-h2c.local/update instead.
